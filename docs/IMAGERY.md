# Sample imagery

Seven images were generated specifically for this demonstration and compressed to WebP. They depict illustrative designs, not manufactured inventory. No external image host is needed.

Art direction: contemporary Indian circular-fashion editorial, warm stone backgrounds, indigo rescued denim, rani-pink brocade, saffron and antique-gold accents. Campaign: adult Indian models in experimental denim and heirloom textiles, natural editorial light. Product imagery: individual front-facing studio garments, warm beige backgrounds, realistic textile detail, no logos or text.

Prompt subjects: utility shacket with brocade panels; denim/magenta saree corset dress; wide-leg cargo trousers; reversible indigo/saffron waistcoat; ink/rust patchwork overshirt; modular brocade/denim wrap. Each image was generated separately and visually inspected before use.
